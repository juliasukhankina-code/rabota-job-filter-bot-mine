# Candidate profile

Senior Product Marketing Manager with experience across product marketing, growth marketing, and go-to-market work, especially for technology products, AI, and SaaS.

## Roles to consider
- Senior Product Marketing Manager / Product Marketing Lead
- Product Marketing Manager for AI, SaaS, or technology products
- Go-to-market / GTM strategy and launch leadership
- Growth marketing leadership closely connected to product positioning and adoption

## Relevant strengths
Product positioning and messaging, market and customer research, go-to-market planning, launches, product adoption, growth strategy, cross-functional work with product, sales, and marketing teams, and translating technical value into clear customer benefits.

## Preferences
Prioritize senior or lead-level roles with meaningful ownership of product positioning, launches, and growth. Consider remote or hybrid roles. Favor AI, SaaS, and technology companies.

## Screen out
Do not prioritize junior roles, pure performance-advertising roles without product marketing ownership, or pure sales positions.

Use the vacancy text to assess responsibilities and seniority, not title alone. Do not invent qualifications that are not stated in this profile.
